# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Overview

Prototype of VMock's adaptive AI interviewer (spec: *Adaptive Interviews v1, Resume + optional JD*). Zero dependencies, Node >= 18. Feedback scoring and rubric are intentionally out of scope.

## Commands

- `npm start` (or `node server.js`): serves app + API at http://localhost:3000 (`PORT` overrides).
- `npm test`: runs `test/run-test.js`, which starts `test/mock-openai.js` and drives a full scripted interview through the real server (`test/run-flow.js`). No real API calls. There is a single end-to-end flow, no per-test runner.
- Config: `.env` (`OPENAI_API_KEY`, optional model and reasoning effort per prompt: `PLANNER_MODEL`/`PLANNER_REASONING_EFFORT` (default gpt-6-sol, medium), `TURN_MODEL`/`TURN_REASONING_EFFORT` (gpt-6-sol, low), `FEEDBACK_MODEL`/`FEEDBACK_REASONING_EFFORT` (gpt-6-sol, low), `GROUNDING_MODEL`/`GROUNDING_REASONING_EFFORT` (follow feedback); defaults from `docs/research/2026-11-14-playground-model-comparison/`; all four are also in the page Settings and a request's `settings`; `OPENAI_BASE_URL`, `MARKET`: `india` default or `us`; a request's `market` field overrides it). Key can also be pasted in the page Settings; held in server memory only.

## Architecture

Two model calls, one code-owned rule engine:

1. **Planner** (once, `prompts/planner.md`): resume + optional JD -> claim inventory, competencies, concerns, slot plan (opening question, what a complete answer needs, planned follow-ups). Output validated by `checkAndShapePlan` in `lib/engine.js` (claims must quote the resume verbatim: a paraphrase is replaced with the closest resume line (`closestResumeLine`), and a topic on an invented line is dropped; max 2 topics per role). Lengths (`SCORED_SLOTS`, `MINUTES`, `MAX_INTERVIEWER_TURNS`): short 3 topics / 10 min, standard 4 / 15, full 6 / 30 (a behavioural round; refused for recruiter screens, hidden in the setup UI; topics 5-6 are extra claim or gap slots before motivation). `market` (`india` or `us`, sent to the planner): in the US the domain-knowledge slot is kept only when the planner's `jd.role_family` is `finance` or `technical` (engineering, data, analytics, any PM role), and `checkAndShapePlan` turns any other US knowledge slot into a situational one; parked US enhancements are in `docs/TODO-US.md`. For MBA students going into business roles (finance, marketing/sales, supply chain/ops, product, consulting, analytics), the hiring manager plan's gap topic becomes a domain-knowledge question (`question_type: knowledge`, judged on the knowledge rubric): an applied decision from the JD, never a definition, guesstimate or case (VMock has separate modules for those), and on a different area from the plan's claim topics so it doesn't re-ask a project. A career switch, including an MBA student's industry change, is always raised once (motivation or concerns); MBA summer-internship topics plan a "what would you do differently" follow-up; JD placeholders such as "a leading FMCG company" are never spoken as the company name. An optional placement stage (`inputs.stage`: summers, finals or laterals; `placement_stage` in the planner input; shown in the room's lobby subtitle) shapes what the plan draws on: summers use pre-MBA work, studies and activities; finals put the summer internship first; laterals ask about scope, trade-offs and why now. One topic comes from a position of responsibility, competition or activity for freshers, in summers, and in every resume deep dive (in a short or standard hiring manager plan with a fit question, the activity takes slot 1 unless a strong pre-MBA job claim does, and then the fit question in slot 2 invites the activity story ("It could be from a club, competition or your job."; not the strengths angle); in a recruiter screen it never replaces slot 2's top-ranked claim: it takes slot 4's claim, and with room for only one claim (a short plan, or slot 4 is concerns) the fit question may ask about it or it is left out; `checkAndShapePlan` warns, never drops, when a summers or fresher plan has no topic from an activity the resume lists, `ACTIVITY_PATTERN`, except in a recruiter screen with only one claim slot or a hiring manager plan whose slot 1 is a strong claim that is not an activity). Standard fit questions (a failure, a disagreement, strengths): exactly one for summers and for freshers with a thin CV (slot 3 in recruiter screens, slot 2 in hiring manager; never in resume deep dives), picked per session by `fitAngle(seed)` and sent to the planner as `fit_angle`; otherwise only when the CV gives nothing better. In a resume deep dive, `focus_lines` (lines the student chose; the server only caps them at 100 × 300 characters) makes every topic a claim on one of those lines, with no concerns or activity topic (two topics on one line each take a different angle, with no overlapping planned follow-ups and only one taking the measurement; once a topic on a line asked how its number was measured (rung `result`), a later topic's opener or follow-up on that line that asks about the measurement again is rewritten once, `asksMeasurement` (attribution counts too: separating, isolating or attributing the effect, ruling out other changes, what would have happened without it, `ATTRIBUTION`); that later topic's turn input says which of the measurement, the candidate's own role and their decisions were asked in another topic on the line (`ELSEWHERE` notes added to its intent, `coveredElsewhere`; measurement probes dropped from its planned follow-ups, `measuredElsewhere`), the engine strips the measurement from its `missing`, and its feedback input lists them under `covered_in_other_topic` (a to-fix or rating reason faulting them, or a role marked not given, is sent back once; `OWN_FAULT` for the role and decisions); `checkAndShapePlan` removes measurement and attribution follow-ups from the second topic on a line (the first keeps them) and warns about them and about such an opening or intent, `plansMeasurement`); the rest of the resume is context only. Each topic has a different competency (`checkAndShapePlan` warns when two share one). A medium-term goal only as a motivation follow-up. The warm-up is one plain spoken question from a single-idea angle (`WARMUP_ANGLES`, `warmupAngle(seed)`) and never assumes whether the candidate is studying or working: it names their job only when the resume marks it current and their studies only with a placement stage. In summers the background walkthrough ends with the MBA so far (covering grades without asking about them; a low CGPA or unrelated degree is an `academics` concern, never asked on its own) and one motivation probe asks what they have done in the MBA towards the domain (any interview type). With a placement stage, recruiter-screen motivation carries one HR-round probe (open to a non-metro or shop-floor posting for sales/field/plant/ops roles, including a domain such as "Sales and Marketing", otherwise other roles or companies being considered); never salary, family or personal circumstances. In laterals, in any interview type, the motivation slot plans a "why now?" probe. The engine owes these (rungs `why_now` in laterals, then `hr_round` (recruiter screens), then in summers, in any interview type, `domain_prep`; `motivationRungs`): the motivation topic can't close until they are asked, its budget is at least one follow-up per owed probe, and the turn input names the planned probe (`owed_follow_up`, matched from `likely_probes` by `PROBE_TEXT`). A gap concern with no concerns slot is named neutrally in the walkthrough. Experience level counts full-time work after graduation only: fresher none (internships only), early up to about 3 years, mid up to 8, senior 9+. When the student asks questions at the end, the feedback adds a moment on whether they were specific or generic (`momentSlotOk`).
2. **Turn** (per answer, `prompts/turn.md`): model proposes a decision for the candidate's answer. After a thin first answer, the first follow-up is open (their part or steps), not a closed measurement question, unless the engine requires it. The student's own questions at the end get a real answer: the JD first, then general, clearly labelled points about this kind of role; company facts the JD doesn't give (stipend, PPO rates, team size) are never invented and go to the recruiter, said as a panel member would ("We'll share the stipend details through the recruiter.").
3. **Engine** (`lib/engine.js`): owns all rules so the model cannot break them: follow-up budgets by style (Supportive 1 / Balanced 2 / Probing 3, +1 on claim topics), second-chance topics (opening written by the engine from the earlier topic's "Tell me about a time…" or "How did you <do X>?" question, else an ask naming the earlier story's subject ("Earlier you told me about the time you <the opening's "I see you…" phrase, or a verb-led CV bullet cut before its result or an amount, with bare counts such as "14" dropped>…"; "Your resume mentions that you…" when the student's answers in that topic never mentioned it), a lead-in sentence naming the skill ("Let's take a different experimentation example.") cut from the model's opening in code, else (rarely) a neutral ask for another example that refers to nothing earlier (`SECOND_CHANCE_NEUTRAL`, exempt from `openerOffLine`), never the skill's name, `secondChanceQuestion`, and an opening about another kind of story is rewritten once, `openerOffLine`; none when the topic's core story was already told in one answer (concrete, with their own part clear), with an outcome or only a measurement nobody asked about missing, or with only an outcome missing that no follow-up asked for once the follow-ups are spent: then the outcome is not `gap_after_budget`), pivots only to later pending topics, never to a knowledge or scenario gap topic (`pivotOptions`), time rule dropping second chances, then trimming follow-ups one at a time from the topic with the most left (never below what a topic still owes), then dropping non-must-haves, then must-haves; required first follow-ups (own role on flagged claims, measurement on number claims; in finals, except recruiter screens, "what would you do differently now?" on an internship topic, which gets one extra follow-up for it; `owedRungs`; the time rule's squeeze keeps the follow-ups a topic still owes; a second topic on the same CV line doesn't owe role or measurement again, gets the earlier topic's opening and follow-ups (`same_line_earlier_topics`), and a role, decision or result follow-up already asked on that line is rejected); a redirect after an abusive, off-purpose or manipulative answer uses no follow-up and isn't listed as one; the first "repeat that" in a topic allows only a rephrase of the same question (no follow-up used); the first "not sure" or plain decline in a scored topic allows only a simpler, narrower version of the same question (clarify) or moving on, never a new or owed follow-up (`DONT_KNOW_NOTE`); that simpler version spends no follow-up while another owed follow-up is still unasked; a second "not sure" or "repeat" in a topic removes follow-ups and clears any owed follow-up (a plain decline such as "No, that's all" counts as "not sure", `DONT_KNOW`, as does a short hedge first ("Honestly I'm not sure") or up to two adverbs ("I really don't know", "I genuinely can't remember", "I really have no idea") or a short answer the model marks `honest_unknown`); after an answer past two minutes (`LONG_SPOKEN_WORDS`), the turn note asks for a short next question and, once per interview (`brevity_asked`), for brevity the way a panel would ("To keep us on time, in a sentence or two: …", `BREVITY_LEAD` is exempt from the option-list guard); the unscored warm-up allows one rephrase only for an unrelated or misheard answer (`on_topic` off), and a warm-up "not sure" moves straight on with "No problem." in place of an opening "Thanks."; up to 2 technical-depth topics. A goodbye under any decision but close or end_session is rejected (`CLOSING_PATTERN`); the first abusive or refusing answer is redirected, never ended. When an opener's wording rewrite changes the decision, a move-on uses the scripted opener. Wording guards (agenda leaks including naming the question type, praise or judging the answer including thanks for a quality of it or a remark on what it gave ("that gives me the timeline", "that's helpful", `THANKS_FOR_QUALITY`; "that comparison makes the saving clear", a sentence opening "You set a clear…"), speaking as if outside the hiring team (`OUTSIDE_TEAM`; company facts go to the recruiter), two asks (joined by "and"/"or what", a tacked-on ", including …?", "the X or your Y?", or two actions after one question word, each with its own object: "How did you measure the change … and attribute it to the redesign?", or passive "How was it calculated and attributed…?" / "calculated and compared"; "How did you design and test it?" is one ask; ", including…" counts on an instruction ending in "." too), leading options, a rephrase or answer-and-return that repeats the last question word for word, an acknowledgement judging what they gave ("The attendance process was clear, …", "That distinction between … is useful.", `ACK_JUDGE`; not in the candidate's questions, where the interviewer describes the role), a follow-up asked as a yes/no question ("Have you done any…?" with no question word, `yesNoQuestion`; "Could you walk me through…" is open), >~60 words) trigger one rewrite of the message; the rewrite's own `probe_rung` is recorded, but a rewrite that swaps an owed follow-up (role, measurement, …) for another is not used: the original is kept and cut to one ask; the result is checked again, and praise or a second ask still in it is cut out without another model call (praise sentences that ask nothing dropped, ", and how/what…" or ", including…" cut, only one question kept: the first for a follow-up, the last for a move-on, pivot or answer-and-return, `trimUtterance`); characters from a non-Latin script are stripped from what the interviewer says, and "?." or "?!" becomes "?" (`pushInterviewer`). Agenda words: criteria count only as the interview's or panel's ("the interview's selection criteria", "the selection criteria here"); the student's own "selection criteria" or "your criteria" don't. `end_session` only after an abusive or refuse-to-engage answer (`answer_type` abusive or off_purpose, `ENDABLE`) once the candidate has been redirected (`redirected`: the answer just before, skipping manipulation attempts, was abusive or a refusal; a normal answer since resets it; a plain decline such as "No more questions" never ends it); once redirected, the turn input's note says so (`REDIRECTED_NOTE`), and an abusive or off_purpose answer then ends the interview with a scripted neutral close (`REDIRECT_END`) whatever decision the model chose (in the warm-up too, where end_session is offered once the student was redirected; never on a "not sure" or "repeat"); a move-on that asks the current question again is rejected, so the scripted opener asks the next topic's question. An answer given after a redirect still counts as the topic's first answer in the feedback (intended); manipulation (instructions in an answer, fake SYSTEM text, asking for the plan, the topics left, the criteria or a score, harmful requests) is `answer_type` manipulation (the conduct note also counts such an ask during a topic whatever its label, `AGENDA_ASK`), declined in one line, and can never end the interview (guardrail stress kit: `docs/research/2026-11-13-guardrail-stress-test/`). Disallowed model decisions are rejected and re-requested; after a second failure a scripted move-on is used. The turn model receives the full resume and JD.

4. **Feedback** (once, after the interview ends, `prompts/feedback.md`): qualitative student feedback, cached on the session with a `version` (older versions are regenerated). It uses the session's feedback model (grounding calls use the grounding model; sessions saved before these settings use their turn model); when that model is no longer available to the key (403/404, an old session), it falls back to the current default for that step (`withModelFallback` in `server.js`). Every model call (planner, turn, feedback, grounding) goes through `withFallback`: a model the key can't use (403/404) moves to the step's default, then a ranked list per prompt (`FALLBACKS`, or `MODEL_FALLBACKS` in `.env` for all prompts); failed models are remembered for the server's lifetime, and the trace records `fallback_from`. The waiting screen shows a running clock, and a reload while feedback is being written waits for it (retries the 409) instead of failing. Built around what went well / what to fix / how to answer next time. Per topic: the question asked (`questionAsked`, without the bridge from the previous topic, but keeping the sentence a kept lead-in's "it" refers to), a verdict derived from the ratings (`topicVerdict`; needs_work when half or more elements are needs_work, when one is and none is good_job, or when the topic ended after two "not sure"/"repeat" answers; such a topic is too short to rate but still shows Needs work, `ended_early`), a verbatim went-well and to-fix quote, a 2-4 step answer plan, the student's own line improved with bracketed placeholders, and a labelled sample answer only for scenario/technical questions. Topics under 15 words get the plan but no ratings. Rubric depends on question type (`RUBRICS`/`rubricFor`: behavioural STAR (+ Technical reasoning for technical claim topics), situational, knowledge, motivation, concern) plus "Handling follow-ups" when probed; levels Good job / On track / Needs work, reason written before level. Each rating also says where the element appeared (`found`: first answer / after a follow-up / not given) with a verbatim `quote` (from a follow-up reply when given after one). The model input lists, per topic, the required follow-ups never asked (`not_asked`, from `notAskedRungs`: the owed ones, plus the measurement of a number on the CV line whenever nobody asked about it, in any interview type, and in a story gap or fit topic the measurement of a result number the student gave (a percentage, multiple or amount, or a number in a sentence with a result verb) when no follow-up asked about it, `studentGaveNumber`); their absence is never the to-fix moment, and a to-fix or a below-good_job rating reason faulting an unasked measurement or attribution is sent back once (`MEASURE_FAULT`; a reason only when it says something was missing, `LACK`). Each topic carries its `follow_ups` (question, time, the rubric element its rung asked for via `rungDimension`, whether it was given: never when the reply to that follow-up was "not sure" or a short non-answer: the model's honest_unknown or no_example counts only under 25 words, so a longer reply quoted by a rating shows as given, `followUpAnswered`), its `segment` in the recording (from transcript times) and, for topics from the JD rather than a resume line, `jd_line` (the JD requirement). Also: a goal for the next attempt (with `slot_id`, the judged topic that shows it) plus up to 2 more priorities (one restating the goal is dropped, `sameItem`), warm-up/background "moments" (model) and "no questions at the end" (derived), JD must-have coverage (derived). `checkAndShapeFeedback` keeps only reached topics, valid levels and same-topic verbatim quotes, and strips scores and agenda vocabulary. Since version 10: no overall line, goal or priorities when no answer was judged (no model call at all when no topic was reached); a reached topic long enough to judge that comes back missing or unrated is sent back once, and the pages say "none long enough to judge" only when every topic was too short; abusive, off-purpose and manipulation answers are left out of quotes and answer length (`candidateTextBySlot(session, { skipNonAnswers })`); went-well and to-fix never quote the same words (sent back once, then the went-well quote goes); a rewrite writes in a number the resume line already gives instead of a bracket (sent back once); no advice to say "I" / use the first person when the student already says "I" far more than "we" (`mostlyI`, `PRONOUN_ADVICE`; such plan steps are dropped). Since version 11: an element marked not given is always needs_work; in behavioural, motivation and concern topics an element given only after a follow-up is at most on_track (enforced in `checkAndShapeFeedback`; a "first answer" whose quote is only in a later reply counts as after a follow-up; technical reasoning, "what you'd do differently", scenario and knowledge topics and the strengths fit question (`isStrengthsFit`) are exempt), and the prompt anchors each level with examples; answers past about two minutes (`LONG_SPOKEN_WORDS` 280 at 140 words a minute, warm-up and end questions excluded) get a derived "Answer length" moment (`answerLength`), and two or more add a "Keep answers under two minutes" priority right after the goal. Since version 12: an abusive answer or a refusal to engage gets a derived "Keeping to the interview" moment (`conduct`, slot_id `conduct`), and the model input's `conduct` tells it not to turn those answers into advice; a rewrite pointing at the resume instead of saying the number ("the amount stated on my resume", `RESUME_POINTER`) is sent back once. Since version 13: a finals-internship topic (`finals_internship` on the slot) whose interviewer asked "what would you do differently now?" (rung `setback`) is rated on a `reflection` element ("What you'd do differently", shown under Learning & Reflection; `rungDimension` maps setback to it); a rewrite keeps "we" for what the student gave as the team's and brackets their own part ("I [what you personally did]"); a rewrite naming the measure the resume line counts ("students" for "attended by 120 students") with no number at all is sent back once; "rate" alone is not a score word ("conversion rate"), and since version 17 "rating"/"rated" count only about the student ("your rating", "rated you", "rated as"; "app rating" is fine); the conduct moment counts the turns ("twice", "more than once"). Since version 14: the resend note asks to keep a level for every rubric element and forbids only numeric scores or grades (a live resend told "Give no scores or ratings." dropped every rating), and the resend replaces the first result only where it is no worse (`pickResend`: with more problems the first is kept whole; a topic that lost its ratings or went missing keeps the first's); a manipulation attempt gets a brief neutral line in the conduct moment and is in the model input's `conduct`; an unasked laterals "why now?" is in `not_asked`, and a to-fix or rating reason faulting it is sent back once; a measurement asked in another topic on the same line is `covered_in_other_topic`; `RESUME_POINTER` allows up to six words before "reported on my resume", and the measure word check also takes the word before "from|by <number>" ("abandonment from 71%"); a goal action with one ", then" ("Pause, then choose…") is one action. Since version 15: a topic rated good_job on every element gets no to-fix, to-fix quote or rewrite (emptied before the send-back checks, so nothing is resent for them); more priorities carry a `slot_id`, and one from such a topic is dropped; a goal on such a topic while another topic has a gap, or a goal about the measurement of a number nobody asked how was measured, is sent back once; the number-drop check counts numbers in words ("six hours", not "day-seven", `HAS_NUMBER`) and fires only on a sentence stating the line's measure with a result verb (`RESULT_VERB`; never on loose words such as "data" or "source", `GENERIC_MEASURE`) or on a bracket naming the measure that changes ("[the reported abandonment change]"); the model input has `placement_stage` and `jd_role_title` (in summers a question about the internship is on target); one abusive or off-topic answer followed by a real one gets a softer conduct line ("then got back on track with your next answer"). Since version 16: any bracket for a quantity ("[starting rate]", "[monthly traffic scale]", `NUMBER_BLANK`) is sent back when the line gives the number, unless the line's number is a total ("together", "combined", `AGGREGATE`, or a count of several things the rewrite names one of) and the bracket names none of its measures; a sentence stating a goal ("The goal was to improve…") or the student's role is not checked as a result (`statesResult`); a topic with an element below good_job and no to-fix (or no verbatim to-fix quote) is sent back once; a goal action that still has two actions after the resend keeps the first (`firstAction`); in a gap or fit story with no follow-up asking it, how the outcome was attributed to the change is in `not_asked`, and a to-fix or reason faulting it ("not clearly isolated") is sent back once; a topic that asked the role or decision itself is never told its own part was covered in another topic on the line; a second chance asked with the neutral opener is exempt from the after-a-follow-up cap, and the model is told its opener named no kind of work. Since version 17 (fewer resends: mechanical fixes are made in code): a goal with two actions is cut to the first in code, never sent back; "probe"/"probing" in feedback text is reworded ("has to ask", "follow-up", `deprobe`) instead of tripping the agenda filter; a quantity bracket counts only when it names the line's measure or the number's noun (up to three words after a number) or is a bare quantity ("[amount]", "[starting rate]", `BLANK_FILLER`), so "[historical leakage amount]" for an input the line doesn't give passes; the empty to-fix check uses the model's own levels (a level lowered only by the after-a-follow-up cap needs no fix); a decision covered by another topic on the line is also not faulted as a rejected alternative or trade-off (`DECISION_FAULT`); a further priority whose topic may not carry its fault (unasked or covered measurement, attribution, why now, own part) is dropped in code; second-chance topics get `not_asked` like gap topics. Since version 18: a follow-up reply the turn judged general, or "we" on a role or action follow-up, or an element rated needs_work, is never shown as given (`followUpVague`); a topic on track only through the after-a-follow-up cap (the model rated every element good_job) gets an engine-written to-fix with no model call ("You gave the result only when asked. Put it in your first answer.", quoting the student's follow-up reply, with no rewrite); a second-chance topic the time rule dropped gets a derived moment (slot_id `second_example`: "We ran out of time before a second example for <skill>; prepare another story for it.") and the earlier topic's to-fix says it was not revisited (`not_revisited`); the prompt picks the goal by what would most change this student's next interview (a mild own-part pattern gives way to a more specific weakness), and the end-questions moment names the student's actual question and a sharper version for this role. Product decision: in a story question a missing core element (situation, task, action, result) can be faulted even when no follow-up asked for it, since a complete story gives it unprompted; only extra details (how a number was measured, attribution, a rejected alternative, why now, reflection) are protected when unasked. `feedback_trace` keeps each feedback call's `problems` (the first call's were sent back) and, on the resend, which result was `kept`. Each rewrite gets a grounding call (`prompts/grounding.md`); `applyGrounding` ignores flags that are the student's/resume's/JD's own words and removes only the sentence with an unsupported claim; a rewrite that then lost more than half its sentences, or is down to one sentence sharing no word with its to-fix, is emptied.

Other pieces:
- `server.js`: plain `http` server; `/api/` answers only Host localhost/127.0.0.1/[::1] or `ALLOWED_HOSTS` (DNS rebinding) and refuses non-GET calls with a foreign or `null` Origin (CSRF); REST under `/api/` (`config`, `key`, `samples`, `sessions`, `sessions/:id/answers|end|trace`) plus static files from `public/`.
- `lib/openai.js`: Chat Completions client with Structured Outputs and retries; reasoning effort is sent to o-series and gpt-5-or-later models (`REASONING_MODEL`) and dropped on retry if the model rejects it. `lib/schemas.js`: strict JSON schemas for all four calls (planner, turn, feedback, grounding). Turn and feedback schemas are built per call (`turnSchemaFor`, `feedbackSchemaFor`) so the output can only name the decisions allowed this turn, offered pivot topics, reached topics and their rubric dimensions; the engine guards still re-check. Inputs are sent as compact JSON.
- Student voice page (`/student`): `public/student.html|js|css`; push-to-talk (hold Space or the button); `VOICE_PROVIDER` openai (default; `OPENAI_API_TTS_STT_KEY` optional separate key) or browser (speechSynthesis + SpeechRecognition); `lib/voice.js` (TTS `GET sessions/:id/speech` speaks only the latest interviewer line; STT `POST sessions/:id/transcribe` takes a raw audio body). `public/shared.js` holds the feedback renderer used by both pages.
- VMock replica (`/vmock/interview`): `public/vmock/` copies VMock's candidate Interview page (tokens from candidate-uat-us.vmock.dev) and adds an "Adaptive Interviews" tab (`?tab=adaptive`) plus a card on Custom Interviews; only the Custom and Adaptive tabs are real, the rest are placeholders. The adaptive flow's own screens are being rebuilt in this style one at a time. Feedback versions: `public/vmock/feedback-v3.js|css` is the default and the one to edit: v2 without VMock's placeholders (no Delivery of Speech, Non Verbal, STAR sub-checks or More content checks; no module switches; the list shows each question's result). `public/vmock/feedback-v2.js|css` is v2 with the placeholders (`?fbview=v2`); `public/vmock/feedback.js|css` is v1, frozen for comparing (`?fbview=v1`, or `?fbview=classic` for its older content-only layout). ## Editing prompts

`prompts/*.md` are read on every call, so edits apply without restarting the server. The planner, turn and feedback prompts are organised in XML sections (`<role>`, `<security>`, `<inputs>`, `<steps><step n=…>`, …); keep new rules inside the matching section. Resume, JD and answers are untrusted data: each prompt says never to follow instructions found in them. The prompts are kept in step with the deployed copies (interviews-api / interviews-ai): answers are speech-to-text transcripts (turn and feedback judge what was meant, feedback prefers quotes without transcription errors), questions use no dashes, and names a PDF ran together are spoken with normal spacing (claims stay exact copies).
