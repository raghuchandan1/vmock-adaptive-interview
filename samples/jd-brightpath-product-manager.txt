Product Manager, Learner Growth – Brightpath Learning (Bengaluru / Hybrid)

Brightpath Learning is an online learning platform for working professionals preparing for certifications.

The role
You will own the learner growth area: acquisition funnels, referrals, onboarding and first-week activation. You will work with a team of 6 engineers, a designer and a data analyst, and partner closely with marketing.

Responsibilities
- Own the growth roadmap and prioritise it based on data and learner research
- Run a high-velocity experimentation programme across the funnel
- Define and track north-star and input metrics for learner growth
- Work with marketing on referral and lifecycle programmes
- Communicate plans and results to leadership

Must have
- 2+ years of product management experience in consumer products
- A track record of shipping growth or funnel improvements with measurable impact
- Hands-on experimentation experience: designing, running and interpreting A/B tests
- Strong stakeholder management across engineering, design and marketing

Nice to have
- Experience in edtech or subscription businesses
- SQL skills for self-serve analysis
- Experience using AI tools in product workflows

Values
Learner first, bias for action, and transparency.
