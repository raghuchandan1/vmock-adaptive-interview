Product Analyst (Graduate hire) – Lumen Pay, Bengaluru

About Lumen Pay
Lumen Pay builds UPI-first payment and credit products for small merchants across India.

What you'll do
- Analyse product and funnel data to find where merchants drop off during onboarding and activation
- Build and maintain dashboards that product managers use to make weekly decisions
- Define success metrics for new features with product managers and measure their impact
- Design and analyse A/B tests with the experimentation team
- Present insights clearly to product, design and operations teams

Must have
- Strong SQL and comfort working with large, messy datasets
- Experience with a BI tool such as Power BI, Tableau or Looker
- Structured problem solving: breaking down an ambiguous question into a clear analysis
- Clear written and verbal communication

Nice to have
- Python for analysis
- Exposure to experimentation or A/B testing
- Interest in fintech or payments

Our values
Customer obsession, ownership, and candour.
