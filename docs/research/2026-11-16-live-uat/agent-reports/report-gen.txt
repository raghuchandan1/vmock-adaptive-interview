1. ENTRY PATH MAP
==================================================================
Start: Dashboard /dashboard/smart-interviews/practice/career-interviews
Click "+ Generate Interview" (also listed under Custom Interviews). A full-screen wizard opens with steps 1, 2, 3.

STEP 1  "Upload Job Description"
  Subtitle: "If you have a particular job in mind, type/paste the job description here"
  Inputs:
  - Text box, placeholder "Type job description here".
  - "Attach Job Description" button; the file picker accepts PDF only.
    After attaching, the button reads "Update Attachment" and the PDF's text appears in the box.
  - "Previously added job descriptions": a JD used before shows as a card; clicking it fills the box.
  Buttons: Skip, Next. Next is disabled while the box is empty.
  Validation: a 3-word JD ("hello test test") shows, in red:
    "The job description does not have enough content!"
  While Next runs: "Please wait! Processing Job Description ..."

STEP 2  "Select Resume"
  - JD skipped: subtitle "You skipped the job description, so pick a resume to build your interview from".
    There is no Skip button, so a resume is required.
    The newest resume is preselected; clicking it again does not unselect it.
  - List: columns Name / Upload/Time / Module, a search box "Search by resume name",
    "+ Upload New Resume (N left)", and a preview on the right.
  - Account with no resume: "Upload Resume / Drop your PDF file here / or / Choose a File / 10 uploads left".
  - JD given and resume skipped: goes to the old question set ("Interview Questions": Q1-Q7 at 120 sec,
    "+ Add Question", "Start Practicing"). CareerBuddy is never offered on that path.
  - After the first format choice, the footer shows: Back, "Switch format", "Start with CareerBuddy".

FORMAT CHOOSER  "How do you want to practice?"
  Options:
  - "Practice with CareerBuddy" (NEW / Current): "CareerBuddy asks out loud and follows up on what you say."
  - "Record at your pace": "See the questions first, then record an answer to each one."
  Without a JD, two fields are required:
  - "Target role" (placeholder "For example: Product Analyst")
  - "Function": Consulting, Finance & BFSI, Human Resources, IT & Analytics,
    Operations & Supply Chain, Product Management, Sales & Marketing,
    Strategy & General Management, Sustainability & ESG
  - Hint: "There's no job description, so CareerBuddy needs a role and a function."
  - "Start with CareerBuddy" stays disabled with only a role, with only a function,
    or with a role of only spaces.
  - The footer "Start with CareerBuddy" behind the chooser is always enabled,
    but without a JD it reopens the chooser instead of starting.
  "Change length": About 10 min / About 15 min (default 15).
  Not offered anywhere in this flow: interview type (always "Hiring manager round"),
  placement stage, style, or a 30-minute option.

LOBBY  "Ready to start?"
  Example: "Data Analyst · Hiring manager round / 10 minutes · 3 topics, then your questions"
  Then "THIS PANEL LOOKS FOR" (4 items), "QUICK TIPS" (3 items), progress lines, "Start conversation".

ROOM  /interviews/adaptive/room/<id>
  Typing: click "Switch to text mode" (the mic icon) and a box appears ("Ask me anything about your career.....").
  Enter sends; the Send button sends; Send is disabled when the box is empty.
