CareerBuddy adaptive interviews: UAT round 2

Live UAT, dashboard-uat-us.vmock.dev 21 Nov 2026 (round 1: 16 Nov) Mock Interviews · Generate Interview · Resume Deep Dive 7 testers, 7 accounts, 24 interviews (268–303), typed answers
Much better, still not ready to release

Every round-1 Blocker in the interviewer is fixed: no criteria leak, no stuck "Thinking", unreached topics are no longer rated, and abuse and "not sure" are handled correctly. The questions are close to a real panel. What still blocks release:

  1. Feedback contradicts itself. Scores and levels disagree with the text, and an interview ended for abuse still gets "Communication & Presence 100/100".
  2. Voice mode by default. The room starts and rejoins with the mic live, so background sound is submitted and graded.
  3. Role questions go to the recruiter when there is no JD, and the feedback then blames the student for asking.
  4. Setup dead ends. A nonsense JD still spins forever, Generate Interview is still unreachable on a phone, and a JD with no resume silently drops CareerBuddy.
