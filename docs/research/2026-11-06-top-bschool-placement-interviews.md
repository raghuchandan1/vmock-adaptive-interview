# Placement interviews at the top Indian B-schools: format, preparation and what to change in our interviewer

Research note, 6 November 2026. Extends [What IIM students are asked in placement interviews](2026-11-06-iim-placement-interview-questions.md) (the "earlier note") and [Do our interview questions fit IIM placements?](2026-11-06-iim-placement-question-fit.md) (the "fit note"). The earlier note covered question content per domain. This note covers the interview format, how students prepare, what they get wrong, how freshers, summers, finals and laterals differ, and communication expectations. Schools: IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta, Lucknow, Kozhikode, Indore; ISB; XLRI; FMS Delhi; SPJIMR; JBIMS; MDI; IIFT; IIT Bombay SJMSOM. Cases and guesstimates stay out of scope: VMock has separate Case Interview and Guesstimate modules.

## Summary

- **The personal part of a placement interview is short and comes first.** IIM Bangalore's consulting club gives it about 5 minutes before a 20-30 minute case. FMS's club gives the behavioural part 5-15 minutes. IIM Calcutta accounts describe whole rounds of 5 to 25 minutes, often with 2-3 rounds in a row. Students say the first minutes of personal questions "set the tone".
- **"Tell me about yourself" opens almost every round, sometimes with a time limit.** Examples: "Tell me about yourself in 1 minute" (BCG) and "Explain your PORs in 2 mins" (McKinsey), both at IIM Calcutta. A Bain candidate who ran over on his introduction "had already been interrupted". We found no evidence of a "30 seconds per CV point" rule.
- **The CV is the question bank, and any line can be asked.** Interviewers ask about each line under academics, PORs, hobbies (dance, HAM radio, photography, dramatics, table tennis), hometown and school. Some say "Walk me through your CV", "Tell me any 3 things from your CV which you would like to discuss" or ask for "two great things I feel about my resume". Students advise: "Know your CV in and out", and back every point with 2-3 examples.
- **Panels cut in, push back and test for prepared answers.** Examples: "stopped me midway", "Let's forget the preliminaries, just tell me your approach", "Give me the real reasons and not prepared", "Why should I not select you?". Prep guides warn that rehearsed-sounding answers hurt.
- **A recurring lesson in the IIMC consulting accounts is not to neglect the personal round.** They say: "Don't neglect your personals", "Never ignore personals", "Prepare personals very well". The other recurring lessons are research on the firm and having a good question ready for the interviewer.
- **Students practise with peers, seniors and placement committees.** That means mock interviews, buddy cases and fixed lists of fit questions that placement committees circulate. We found almost nothing on what feedback those mocks give.
- **Experience changes the process more than the questions.** Laterals (for example 22+ months at IIM Bangalore) and the one-year programmes (ISB, IIMA PGPX) have separate or rolling processes for mid-level roles. We found no first-hand account of what laterals or one-year students are asked.
- **Communication is judged throughout.** The expectations are crisp, structured, consistent and genuine answers. One IIMB placement chair writes that "companies expect students to have neutral accents". We found no recruiter's own comment on accent.

**The main product implications are a timed self-introduction, a CV sweep that reaches PORs, hobbies and academics, a choice of placement stage in setup, short placement-day rounds, a pushback style, and better handling of long answers.** See "Improvements for the current product" at the end.

### Two biases in this evidence

1. **Date.** Most first-hand material is from 2013-2019:
   - IIM Calcutta batch graduating 2018
   - IIM Lucknow 2018 summers
   - JBIMS 2017-19
   - IIM Indore, FMS and IIFT in 2013
   - SJMSOM in 2016
   The IIMB (2022-23) and FMS (2021-22) casebooks and the ISB (2023) and InsideIIM (2024) pieces are newer.
2. **Domain.** The detailed evidence on personal questions, interruptions and CV-line grilling comes almost entirely from consulting casebooks. We extend it to FMCG and finance only where an FMCG or finance account backs it: HUL, Coca-Cola, JBIMS, and the XLRI note on P&G, HUL, TAS and ITC. Casebooks also collect accounts from students who got the offer, not from those who failed.

### Evidence labels

The labels follow the earlier note, plus one new one:

- **[First-hand, read]:** a student's own account. We read the page. Quotes are verbatim or near-verbatim, because pages passed through a summariser. Club casebooks are first-hand compilations, but the copies we read were third-party uploads (pdfcoffee, SlideShare).
- **[Official, read]:** an institute or recruiter page that we read.
- **[Search summary only]:** we saw only a search engine's summary of the page. The page was blocked (HTTP 403/500) or we did not fetch it. These items are paraphrased and never put in quotation marks. Check them before relying on them.
- **[First-hand, unverified]:** a Glassdoor or AmbitionBox review seen only as a search summary. None is used here.
- **[Secondary]:** prep sites, coaching sellers, aggregators.
- **Excluded:** admission-interview material. This includes IIM Calcutta's 15-25 minute admission panels, rapid-fire technical questions in an IIMA admission interview, interruptions in IIM Kozhikode and CAP admission interviews, XLRI HRM, MDI and SPJIMR WAT-PI accounts, and a Glassdoor XLRI "12-15 minute" entry we could not place. Most search results for these schools were admission interviews, and we kept them all out.

## 1. Interview format on placement days

### Process and calendar

- **IIM Ahmedabad (summers) runs a cluster-cohort process.** "Firms were grouped into cohorts based on the industry they operate in", and the cohorts were invited to campus across three clusters. Students can make "dream" applications to a later cluster while holding an offer. For the class of 2027, more than 165 firms offered over 230 roles across 25 cohorts, and 410 students were placed ([IIMA media release, Nov 2025](https://www.iima.ac.in/inmedia/consolidatedmedia-release-summer-placement-2025-26)) [Official, read].
  - Summers were fully virtual in 2020 and hybrid (in person or virtual) in 2022 ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/iima-placement-all-388-students-placed-131-recruiters-participated/amp), [IIMA](https://www.iima.ac.in/inmedia/pgp-summer-placements-2024-2025-consolidated-media-release)) [Search summary only].
- **IIM Calcutta also runs clusters and cohorts.** It has Hold & Wait and Dreams rules. Its 2020 summers were virtual, with students at home ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/iim-calcutta-summer-placements-100-students-placed-get-570-offers/amp)) [Search summary only].
- **Laterals are a separate process.** IIM Bangalore requires 22 months of work experience for lateral placements, and "it is different across IIMs" ([Ganesh N Prabhu, IIMB, then Chairperson of Career Development Services, InsideIIM 2019](https://insideiim.com/dream-job-b-school-iim-bangalore-professor)) [Secondary, read; written by IIMB's faculty placement chair].
  - IIMA ran its 2025 lateral placements separately, on 25 January ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/iim-ahmedabad-pgp-placements)) [Search summary only].
  - FMS runs laterals in December and the main final process in January ([Cracku](https://cracku.in/fms-delhi-placements-2026/)) [Search summary only; secondary].
- **ISB runs Day 0 and Day 1, "with a week's breather in between".** The whole process "wraps up in a concise 15-day window". The gap between shortlist and interview is "relatively tight", and "you can't interview for all, so prioritization is key" ([Karan Dhir, ISB, Dec 2023](https://koffeeguy.substack.com/p/placements-season-at-isb)) [First-hand, read].
  - ISB's recruiter policies ask firms to share their interview process 7 days in advance. They ask firms not to make audio or video recordings of interviews and refer to an SOP for online interviews. PPIs are scheduled with the firm's regular interviews ([ISB recruiter policies](https://placement.isb.edu/recruiter/policies.aspx)) [Search summary only; the page returned HTTP 500].
- **IIMA's one-year PGPX** runs a wave process in November, then a rolling process from December, aimed at middle and senior roles ([IIMA PGPX careers](https://www.iima.ac.in/academics/MBA-PGPX/careers-and-placements)) [Search summary only].
- **SPJIMR's summers process was run in person.** Pre-placement talks start about 45 days before placement week ([SPJIMR report 2025](https://www.spjimr.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/PGDM-PGDMBM-Report-2025.pdf), [brochure](https://www.spjimr.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/pgdm-summer-internship-brochure-2024.pdf)) [Search summary only].
- **FMS ran its 2025-27 summers in hybrid mode** ([Cracku](https://cracku.in/fms-delhi-placements-2026/)) [Search summary only; secondary].

**What this means for format:** since 2020, placement interviews are in person, by video or by phone. An Accenture MD round at IIM Lucknow "went on for around 20 min over phone" ([IIML Casebook 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read]. Our camera-on voice interview fits all three.

### Length and number of rounds

- **IIM Bangalore consulting club (2022-23):** "Personality based ques. (5 min); Case discussion (20-30 min); Closing ques. for interviewer (2 min)" ([ICON casebook](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)) [First-hand, read].
- **FMS consulting club (2021-22):** the stages are general discussion 1-2 min, behavioural questions 5-15 min, guesstimate 10-15 min, cases 20-40 min and wrap-up 3-5 min. "There could be multiple case rounds with different partners" ([FMS casebook](https://www.slideshare.net/ssuserd4c2b2/the-fms-consulting-casebook-202122pdf)) [First-hand, read].
- **IIM Calcutta consulting summers (batch graduating 2018):**
  - McKinsey candidates usually had 2 rounds, some had 3, and BCG candidates had 1-2.
  - One round lasted about 25 minutes, one 6-7 minutes, and one was "a short five minute case".
  - One candidate's third round, with a partner, was entirely personal questions, followed by an offer.
  - Interviewers were usually one Partner, Senior Partner or Principal per round, so most rounds were one-to-one.
  - One BCG candidate said the first 5 minutes of personal questions "set the tone for the entire interview". A Bain candidate said personal questions took more than half his interview time.
  - Source: [IIMC compilation](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
- **IIM Lucknow summers 2018:** an A&M candidate's first 15 minutes were a CV discussion of his PORs ([IIML Casebook 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read].
- **The earlier note's IIML casebook:** about 20 minutes per interview in summers and about 45 in finals.
- **Non-consulting examples:**
  - **Coca-Cola, IIM Kozhikode, Day 0, 2021:** about 50 students were shortlisted from the CV. Two rounds tested "brand knowledge and marketing skills" and the HR side, and 8 reached the final interviews ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-coca-cola-devyani-bhardwaj-iim-kozhikode)) [First-hand, read].
  - **PwC US Advisory, SJMSOM IIT Bombay finals, 2016:** four rounds. Round 2 "went on for about an hour", and round 3 was "the toughest and longest interview I had faced till date" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/being-average-part-2-my-journey-at-sjmsom-iit-bombay)) [First-hand, read].
- **Volume per student:** at IIM Indore one student had "30+ shortlist and 20+ interviews". An FMS student's shortlists reached 18 before she was placed (both 2013, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/shortlists-interviews-and-offer-letters)) [First-hand, read].

**Takeaway:** a shortlisted student does several short rounds, often on the same day. Each round opens with a few minutes of personal and CV questions. Finals rounds and non-consulting final rounds can run much longer.

### "Tell me about yourself" and timed answers

- **The opener in most IIM Calcutta consulting accounts is "Tell me about yourself" or "Walk me through your CV".** Timed variants:
  - "Tell me about yourself in 1 minute" (BCG).
  - "Explain your PORs in 2 mins" (McKinsey).
  - One Bain candidate ran over on his introduction and "had already been interrupted".
  - Source: [IIMC compilation](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
- **ISB:** "Have a well-thought-out response to the classic 'Tell me about yourself'" ([Dhir](https://koffeeguy.substack.com/p/placements-season-at-isb)) [First-hand, read].
- **JBIMS finance summers (2017-19):** common openers were walk me through your CV and tell me something which is not in your CV ([JBIMS student compilation on Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/790256938/Summer-Interview-Experiences-2017-2019-Batch)) [Search summary only; the page did not load].
- **Not found:** any placement evidence for "explain this CV point in 30 seconds" or for rapid-fire CV questions. The closest are the 1-minute and 2-minute prompts above. Rapid-fire questions in search results came from admission interviews.

### Interruptions, pushback and stress

All examples are from the IIM Calcutta compilation unless another source is linked [First-hand, read]:

- **Interviewers cut in to ask for brevity:**
  - "stopped me midway… and asked for 3 actionable things" (McKinsey)
  - "Let's forget the preliminaries, just tell me your approach" (BCG)
  - "just give me your methodology since we are running out of time" (BCG)
  - "Do not get into the details. Just give me a methodology" (Bain)
  - "regular interjections" (Bain)
- **Stress rounds:**
  - "a pure stress test" with "very little to no help" (McKinsey).
  - A BCG interviewer rebutted nearly every idea.
- **Challenge questions:**
  - "What makes you a better candidate than the rest of the people I am seeing today?" (McKinsey)
  - "Would people describe you as an aggressive person?" (McKinsey)
  - "Are you sure you want to take this chance?" (Bain, on the PPO bar)
  - "Why should I not select you?" and, from an MD, "Give me the real reasons and not prepared" (Accenture, [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html))
- **Counter-questions on career choices (JBIMS):** one student was asked why he did engineering and not CA, then cross-questioned again after answering. The advice was to be clear why you want finance ([JBIMS compilation](https://www.scribd.com/document/790256938/Summer-Interview-Experiences-2017-2019-Batch)) [Search summary only].
- **The earlier note's Deloitte account (IIM Lucknow)** is a deliberate stress HR round, including "In your CV, I don't find any POR on campus?".
- **Not every panel is tough:** several BCG rounds were described as friendly or conversational, and the Coca-Cola interview "seemed to me like a conversation".

## 2. What students are told to prepare

### The CV as the question bank

- **Every line can be asked:**
  - A Kearney partner "Asked about each line mentioned under academics in my CV".
  - "95% of the interview based on work experience mentioned on CV" (Accenture).
  - "Tell me any 3 things from your CV which you would like to discuss" (Accenture).
  - Questions came from hobbies on the CV: dancing (Kearney), a HAM radio club (Kearney), photography samples (Accenture), table tennis (Accenture).
  - Source: [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
  - More examples: "two great things I feel about my resume" and "Tell me about your dramatics experience" ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read].
- **Students' advice:**
  - "Know your CV in and out." "Whatever written in the CV must be backed by 2/3 examples to add credibility to the point." "They look for consistency in your answers." ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read]
  - "Know your CV well→ personality ques are based on CV to break ice and getting to know you" ([IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)) [First-hand, read].
  - IIMA's consult club says to be ready for any question arising from what the CV mentions: past jobs, internships and projects ([IIMA casebook 2022-23](https://pdfcoffee.com/iima-casebook-2022-23-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read; paraphrased].
  - ISB: "ensure each bullet point has a compelling story to tell, framed in the popular STAR format" ([Dhir](https://koffeeguy.substack.com/p/placements-season-at-isb)) [First-hand, read].
  - JBIMS: know your CV well and prepare for counter-questions on it ([JBIMS compilation](https://www.scribd.com/document/790256938/Summer-Interview-Experiences-2017-2019-Batch)) [Search summary only].

### One-page CV, CV points and verification

- **The one-page CV is a large effort:**
  - It "will take up more than 100 hours", including "at least half of your 1st term".
  - Advice comes "from your Placement committees as well as from as many seniors as you approach".
  - Recruiters spend "not more than 15-20 secs per CV".
  - Source: [Amol Aranake, InsideIIM 2018, tagged IIM Bangalore](https://insideiim.com/cv-prep-summer-placements-much-much) [First-hand, read].
- **Placement committees verify CV points:**
  - First-years are "on their toes for CV points and verification by the placecom" ([InsideIIM, Aug 2024](https://insideiim.com/how-to-prepare-for-mba-summer-placements-preparation-tips)) [Secondary].
  - IIM Lucknow's 2019 tender for placement software includes a verification status for each CV point ([IIML tender](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/tender/corrigendumRFQPlacementAutomation-01August2019.pdf)) [Official; search summary only].
- **IIMB's placement chair:** "Many have only a half-page resume when they join an IIM – such a resume will not work". He tells students to "collect documentary evidence" for every item ([Prabhu](https://insideiim.com/dream-job-b-school-iim-bangalore-professor)) [Secondary, read; by IIMB's faculty placement chair].
- **A sparse CV draws comment.** An SJMSOM student "hardly managed to fill 60 percent of the 1 page CV" and was shortlisted by only three Day 0 companies ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/being-average-part-2-my-journey-at-sjmsom-iit-bombay)) [First-hand, read].
- **Not found:** an official school document setting one-page or numbers-on-CV rules. Templates that say so are coaching sites.

### PORs, extracurriculars and academics

- **PORs:**
  - "Explain your PORs in 2 mins" (McKinsey).
  - "Elaborate on the PORs held. What was one key challenge you faced" (BCG).
  - "What PoRs do you plan on taking up at Joka?" (McKinsey, to a first-year).
  - Source: [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
  - This reinforces fit-note item 6.
- **Academics in summers:**
  - "How was your first term?" and "What is your CGPA at IIMC like and what approximate rank would that be?" (McKinsey).
  - "Why you couldn't make it to IIT?" (McKinsey).
  - Source: [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
- **Personal background:**
  - Hometown, school and family come up: "Where are you from?" and "What is the one thing you like about Kolkata?" (IIMC); "Who all are there in your family?" (Kearney, [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)).
  - Family questions are real but touch private characteristics. Our planner rightly avoids them.

### Why this firm, research and questions for the interviewer

- **Firm-knowledge checks:**
  - "What is the share price of Accenture?" An HR interviewer was annoyed when the candidate did not know it.
  - "How many employees does Accenture have, and what is its net revenue?"
  - "Why A.T.Kearney?"
  - Source: [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
  - Why McKinsey over BCG, and "Why Bain?" ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read].
- **FMCG:** the Coca-Cola candidate had studied "their advertisement, initiatives, brands, markets" and "had formed opinions and comments about everything" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-coca-cola-devyani-bhardwaj-iim-kozhikode)) [First-hand, read].
- **Questions for the interviewer are tested too:**
  - "Ask relevant, non-generic question" ([IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)).
  - Research the firm so you can ask good questions ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html), paraphrased).
  - The earlier note's Deloitte interviewer was disappointed when the candidate had none.

### Current affairs

Current-affairs questions are tied to the candidate's field or recent news:
- start-ups as a bubble, and unit economics (McKinsey, to an ex-OYO candidate)
- recent developments in transfer pricing (BCG, to a candidate who had worked in that field)
- the Visa merger (Kearney)
- news (Arete)

Sources: [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html), [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read]. This reinforces the earlier note's implication 8: keep current-affairs questions short and tied to the CV.

### Internship presentation and PPIs

We found nothing new beyond the earlier note (TAS summer-project presentation; PPOs decided by internship reviews). ISB schedules PPIs alongside the firm's regular interviews [Search summary only].

## 3. What students get wrong, and how they practise

### Common lessons from students and clubs

- **Neglecting the personal round:**
  - "Don't neglect your personals" (McKinsey).
  - "Prepare personals very well" (BCG).
  - "Never ignore personals… Answers must reflect who you really are" (Bain).
  - Source: [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html) [First-hand, read].
- **Sounding rehearsed:**
  - IIMA's club lists being mechanical as a don't: rehearsed-sounding answers about personal experience hurt ([IIMA 2022-23](https://pdfcoffee.com/iima-casebook-2022-23-pdf-free.html)) [paraphrased].
  - "Be genuine, they are smart enough to know when you fake" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)).
  - An Accenture MD asked for "the real reasons and not prepared".
- **Going on too long or off track:**
  - STAR keeps answers "short and crisp and to the point" and stops you getting "off track" ([Srijoni Nandy, XLRI, 2019](https://insideiim.com/behavioral-interview-tips)) [First-hand, read].
  - "Be brief yet effective" ([FMS](https://www.slideshare.net/ssuserd4c2b2/the-fms-consulting-casebook-202122pdf)).
  - The interruptions in section 1 show the cost of running long.
- **Inconsistency:** "They look for consistency in your answers" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)).
- **Interrupting the interviewer:** this is in IIMA's list of don'ts [paraphrased].
- **Poor preparation and choice of firms:**
  - "Lack of preparation and choosing the wrong firms to interview with are common culprits" ([Dhir, ISB](https://koffeeguy.substack.com/p/placements-season-at-isb)).
  - An SJMSOM student with no offer: "I couldn't figure out what had I done wrong" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/being-average-part-2-my-journey-at-sjmsom-iit-bombay)). This is the gap our feedback is meant to fill.

### How students practise

- **Placement committees and seniors run mocks:**
  - An SJMSOM second-year: seniors "had to assist in taking mock interviews and GDs for juniors" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/being-average-part-2-my-journey-at-sjmsom-iit-bombay)) [First-hand, read].
  - IIM Shillong's Placement Preparation Committee and IIM Amritsar's Placement Committee list mock interviews, mock GDs and alumni sessions ([IIM Shillong](https://www.iimshillong.ac.in/student-committees/pgp/placement-preparation-committee/), [IIM Amritsar](https://www.iimamritsar.ac.in/students-corner/committee/placements-committee)) [Official; search summary only]. These are not among the target schools, but the pattern is likely common.
  - ISB's Learning & Development team ran resume reviews and mock interviews before placements in 2013 ([ISB admissions blog](https://blogs.isb.edu/admissionsdirector/2013/07/03/isb-placement-process-behind-the-scenes/)) [Official; search summary only].
- **Peers:** an FMS student's friends "took my mock interview and told me my weakness" ([InsideIIM 2013](https://insideiim.com/shortlists-interviews-and-offer-letters)) [First-hand, read].
- **Fixed question lists:**
  - At IIMB, the placement committee circulates a list of about 40 fit questions near the end of the first term ([ICON casebook on Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/doc/69352624/Icon)) [Search summary only].
  - At IIML, an A&M candidate's questions were "Mostly out of the 20 questions that Disha provides" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read].
  - XLRI: "Maintain a repository where you have all your answers" and "Practice saying the answers aloud. A mirror helps" ([XLRI](https://insideiim.com/behavioral-interview-tips)).
  - Students therefore arrive with prepared answers to a known list. The value of an adaptive interviewer is the follow-up that goes past the prepared answer.
- **Not found:** the content of mock feedback (what seniors or alumni tell students after a mock). This is a gap. Placement committees or students would have to tell us.

## 4. Freshers, work experience, summers, finals, laterals and one-year programmes

- **Freshers and first-years in summers** get questions about the first term, CGPA rank, the undergraduate internship ("Tell me about your undergraduate internship. Describe one problem that you solved", BCG) and PORs they plan to take up ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)) [First-hand, read].
- **Work-experience candidates** get interviews built on that work:
  - "All conversation around my work experience" (Kearney partner, [IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)).
  - Leaving a firm or switching field is questioned: "Why did you leave McKinsey?", "Why consulting after having your startup?", "Why do you want to do consulting after working in Jio?". This reinforces fit-note item 4.
  - Relevant work experience also drives shortlists: an SJMSOM student was shortlisted "on the basis of relevant work experience and other factors".
- **Heavy work experience can count against a candidate in some roles.** An IIFT student found "companies do not prefer people with heavy work experience for the sales and marketing role" ([InsideIIM 2013](https://insideiim.com/shortlists-interviews-and-offer-letters)) [First-hand, read].
- **Older candidates get a seniority question:** "How would you feel working under people much younger than you?" (Bain, IIMC) [First-hand, read].
- **Summers and finals differ:** summers are shorter; finals add the summer internship (earlier note) and can run to hour-long rounds (SJMSOM PwC).
- **Laterals (IIMB 22 months; IIMA separate process) and one-year programmes (ISB, IIMA PGPX):**
  - Laterals are hired for mid-level roles (IIMA InsideIIM item above, search summary only).
  - PGPX aims at middle and senior roles (search summary only).
  - At ISB, "some individuals transition to rolling placements, particularly senior professionals", hired "on a case-by-case basis". Dhir advises including "a few firms from the same industry or function as your pre-MBA company".
  - **We found no first-hand account of what laterals, ISB or PGPX students are asked.** Evidence here is about process only.

## 5. Voice and communication expectations

- **Clubs list communication as a scored skill:**
  - "clarity of thought, articulation & crisp communication" (IIML, earlier note).
  - "Communication and presence" ([FMS](https://www.slideshare.net/ssuserd4c2b2/the-fms-consulting-casebook-202122pdf)).
  - Pressure is used "to assess your poise, self confidence and communication skills" ([IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)).
  - "Consulting interviews are 50% about case solving skills & 50% about soft skills" and interviewers "focussed on body language, how well you can articulate things" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)).
  - All [First-hand, read].
- **Accent and clarity of speech:** IIMB's placement chair tells incoming students to "improve on your language and communication skills if they are weak – specifically if you have unclear speech". He writes that "companies expect students to have neutral accents", but to "avoid getting a false US accent!" ([Prabhu, 2019](https://insideiim.com/dream-job-b-school-iim-bangalore-professor)) [Secondary, read; by IIMB's faculty placement chair]. This shows the expectation exists. It is one person's view from 2019, not a recruiter's statement.
- **Employers globally:** communication was the skill most cited by employers of business graduates in GMAC's 2026 Corporate Recruiters Survey (64%, just ahead of problem-solving) ([GMAC 2026](https://www.gmac.com/-/media/files/gmac/research/employment-outlook/2026-corporate-recruiters-survey/report.pdf)) [Search summary only; global, not India-specific].
- **Not found:**
  - Any recruiter's own comment on Indian students' accents, English fluency or code-mixing in placement interviews.
  - Placement evidence on answer length beyond the timed prompts and interruptions in section 1.
  - Coaching blogs make claims ("IIM panels are comfortable with Indian English") but are about admissions. We did not use them.

## Improvements for the current product

Each item names the part of the product it changes, the evidence, and the size of the change: **small** (prompt edits or a constant), **medium** (a new setup field, slot kind or schema change), **large** (a new mode). Items already in the fit note are not repeated. Where new evidence strengthens one, it says so. Cases and guesstimates stay with VMock's Case Interview and Guesstimate modules.

1. **A timed "Tell me about yourself" opening** (planner background question, turn, feedback).
   - **Today:** the background walkthrough is unscored, gets at most one follow-up, and feedback only notes a resume mismatch or a skipped path (`<moments>` in `prompts/feedback.md`).
   - **Change:** for Indian placement interviews, open with "Tell me about yourself" and a light time cue ("in about a minute"). Add a feedback moment when the introduction runs well past 2 minutes, using the transcript times we already store, or when it never links to the role.
   - **Why:** "Tell me about yourself in 1 minute" (BCG) and "Explain your PORs in 2 mins" (McKinsey); a Bain candidate interrupted for running over; ISB advice to prepare it; students say the first 5 minutes "set the tone" ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html), [Dhir](https://koffeeguy.substack.com/p/placements-season-at-isb)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate, mostly consulting. **Size:** small.
2. **Shorter long-answer threshold and a polite cut-in after the answer** (engine, turn, styles).
   - **Today:** `LONG_ANSWER_WORDS = 350`, about 2.5 minutes of speech. The probing style is defined as "never… interrupting". Push-to-talk makes a true mid-answer interruption impossible.
   - **Change:** lower the threshold (for example about 200 words, roughly 90 seconds) at least in Probing. When it trips, open the next message with a brief cut-in ("Let me stop you there. What was your part?"). An optional on-screen elapsed-time hint is a further step.
   - **Why:** "stopped me midway", "Let's forget the preliminaries", "Do not get into the details" ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)); "short and crisp" ([XLRI](https://insideiim.com/behavioral-interview-tips)); "Be brief yet effective" ([FMS](https://www.slideshare.net/ssuserd4c2b2/the-fms-consulting-casebook-202122pdf)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate, consulting-heavy. **Size:** small.
   - **Product decision:** it softens the "never interrupting" rule, so the team should decide.
3. **A CV sweep that reaches PORs, hobbies and academics** (planner slots; engine plan checks).
   - **Change:** in Back your resume, add a "CV sweep" topic: 2-4 quick questions on lines no other topic uses (a POR, an extracurricular or hobby, an academic line, the undergraduate internship). Each gets at most one follow-up, asking for a specific example or "one challenge you faced".
   - **Code constraint:** `SCORED_SLOTS` (3 short / 4 standard) and the max-2-topics-per-role check in `checkAndShapePlan` would crowd it out. It needs its own slot kind, unscored or lightly scored. A smaller first step: let the planner choose a POR or extracurricular claim as one regular topic. This reinforces fit-note item 6 with new evidence.
   - **Why:** "Asked about each line mentioned under academics", hobby questions, "Tell me any 3 things from your CV", "Explain your PORs in 2 mins", "Know your CV in and out… backed by 2/3 examples" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html), [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)).
   - **Evidence:** strong for consulting, moderate elsewhere. **Size:** medium.
4. **Placement stage in setup: Summers, Finals, Laterals, One-year MBA** (setup, planner inputs, length defaults).
   - **Change by stage:**
     - Summers: first term, CGPA, undergraduate internship, PORs, shorter interview.
     - Finals: summer-internship deep-dive (fit-note item 5).
     - Laterals and one-year: scope, leading people, fit for mid-level roles, "working under people much younger than you", and why this function given the pre-MBA industry.
   - **Today:** the planner's `experience_level` adapts sources but not the stage.
   - **Why:** first-term, CGPA and undergraduate-internship questions to first-years ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)); lateral and one-year processes for mid-level roles (section 4, search summary only); summer-internship questions in finals (earlier note).
   - **Evidence:** moderate for summers and finals; weak for laterals and one-year content (process only). **Size:** medium.
5. **"Why this firm" with one specific follow-up, and feedback on the student's own questions** (planner motivation slot, turn, feedback).
   - **Change:**
     - When the JD names a real company, the motivation topic asks why this firm over a peer.
     - A follow-up asks for one specific thing about the firm (a brand, business, recent move).
     - Feedback rates the candidate's end questions as generic or specific. Today it only derives "no questions at the end".
     - Never quiz numbers such as a share price.
   - **Why:** "Why McKinsey over BCG", the Accenture share-price and headcount questions, Coca-Cola research, "Ask relevant, non-generic question" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html), [IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516), [Coca-Cola](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-coca-cola-devyani-bhardwaj-iim-kozhikode)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate. **Size:** small (prompts) to medium (a new feedback field).
6. **A "placement panel" pushback style** (styles, turn prompt).
   - **Change:** courteous but sceptical. It may ask "What's the real reason?", challenge a career choice once ("Why engineering and not CA?"), or ask a challenge question ("Why should we pick you over others today?"). It also checks consistency with earlier answers.
   - **Conflict:** `prompts/planner.md` bans "classic weakness questions", and the probing style rules out hostility. Keep it non-hostile and decide whether challenge questions are allowed.
   - **Why:** "Give me the real reasons and not prepared", "Why should I not select you?" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html)); "What makes you a better candidate than the rest…?", "Are you sure you want to take this chance?" ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate (consulting first-hand; JBIMS search summary only). **Size:** small to medium.
7. **Follow up prepared-sounding answers** (turn, feedback).
   - **Change:** when a motivation or fit answer is generic and could apply to anyone, the next message asks for one specific moment or "the real reason" before moving on. Feedback names it as sounding prepared, without saying "rehearsed".
   - **Why:** students arrive with answers to a 20-40 question list. Clubs warn that rehearsed answers hurt, and an MD asked for "the real reasons and not prepared" ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html), [IIMA 2022-23](https://pdfcoffee.com/iima-casebook-2022-23-pdf-free.html)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate. **Size:** small. This reinforces fit-note item 7 (generic motivation answer) and widens it to all fit answers.
8. **Allow a small set of standard fit questions** (planner rules).
   - **Change:** let the planner use one standard fit question when the CV gives no better behavioural topic: strengths, long-term goal, "a time you took a stance and stuck to it", a teammate not pulling their weight, a leadership example. Our gap slots already ask "tell me about a time…". The new part is strengths, weaknesses and long-term goals. Today `prompts/planner.md` bans "classic weakness questions"; it does not mention strengths or goals, which the planner simply never chooses.
   - **Why:** these appear in IIMC and IIML accounts ("What are your 3 strengths and weaknesses?", "What is your long term goal?", "How do you define success?") and in placement-committee lists ([IIML 2018](https://pdfcoffee.com/iiml-casebook-2018-pdf-free.html), [IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate. **Size:** small. Weigh it against the earlier decision to avoid canned questions.
9. **Short placement-day rounds and a "next round" flow** (interview length, setup, session flow).
   - **Change:**
     - Add a 5-7 minute "personals round" length: introduction plus 2 topics plus one candidate question.
     - Add a "next round" button that starts a second interview on the same CV with a different type or style.
     - Have the next round avoid topics already asked; this needs topic exclusion across sessions.
     - Point to VMock's Case Interview and Guesstimate modules for the case part of a real round.
   - **Today:** `MINUTES` is 10/15 and each session is independent.
   - **Why:** the 5-minute personal part ([IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)), 5-25 minute rounds and 2-3 rounds in a row ([IIMC](https://pdfcoffee.com/iimc-53rd-batch-consulting-interview-experiences-1-pdf-free.html)), 20+ interviews per student ([InsideIIM 2013](https://insideiim.com/shortlists-interviews-and-offer-letters)).
   - **Evidence:** moderate. **Size:** medium.
10. **Voice: test Indian-English speech recognition and pass answer timings to VMock's Delivery module** (voice, integration).
    - **Do not** score accent: speech analysis is out of scope here and belongs to VMock's Delivery of Speech module.
    - **Change:**
      - Run STT accuracy checks on Indian-English recordings, including code-mixing. The fit note lists this as unchecked.
      - Expose per-answer duration and words per minute so Delivery can comment on brevity.
    - **Why:** communication is scored in every club guide. The IIMB chair's "neutral accents" remark shows the expectation exists. GMAC 2026 ranks communication first globally ([Prabhu](https://insideiim.com/dream-job-b-school-iim-bangalore-professor), [GMAC 2026](https://www.gmac.com/-/media/files/gmac/research/employment-outlook/2026-corporate-recruiters-survey/report.pdf), [IIMB ICON](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/casebook-of-iim-bangalore-which-was-free/270707516)).
    - **Evidence:** weak to moderate. **Size:** small (test) to medium (integration).
11. **Tell students what real placement days add** (pre-brief or help text). Rounds are short and back-to-back, may be by phone or video, and often have a GD or case before or after (fit-note item 8). We can't simulate these. **Evidence:** moderate. **Size:** small.

Already a strength, no change needed: consistency with the resume (`resume_mismatch`, the clarify-on-conflict rule) matches "They look for consistency in your answers". CV-claim deep-dives with "your own part" and "how was it measured" match "backed by 2/3 examples".

- Casebooks contain successful candidates' accounts only, uploaded by third parties.
- No first-hand placement-interview content for MDI, IIFT (beyond one line), SPJIMR, DMS IIT Delhi or XLRI HRM. Searches returned admission interviews instead.
- No account of what laterals, ISB or IIMA PGPX students are asked; process only.
- No evidence for "explain this CV point in 30 seconds" or rapid-fire CV rounds in placements. No official one-page CV rule.
- Nothing on the content of mock-interview feedback at any school.
- Accent and English fluency rest on one placement chair's 2019 opinion and a global survey. No recruiter's own statement found.
