Area Sales Manager – a leading FMCG company (Pune / Field role)

About the role
We are hiring an Area Sales Manager for western Maharashtra, with Pune as the base. You will own primary and secondary sales for personal care and home care brands across modern trade and general trade.

Responsibilities
- Manage and coach a team of 8 sales officers and distributor salesmen
- Grow distributor reach and numeric distribution in tier-2 and tier-3 towns
- Plan monthly targets, schemes and trade promotions with the regional manager
- Track sales, collections and stock levels using the DMS and weekly reviews
- Build relationships with key distributors and modern trade buyers

Requirements
- MBA in sales or marketing, or 2+ years of field sales experience
- Comfort with travel across the region and working with distributors
- Strong numbers sense and the ability to read sales data
