AI [s2] You mentioned duplicate records and overlapping promotion periods. What SQL logic did you use to decide which price record was valid?
ME [s2] I first defined validity rules with category and engineering teams. A record had to be active for the transaction timestamp, match the relevant seller and SKU, and pass basic checks such as a non-null price and valid promotion dates. For duplicates, I used a window function, partitioning by seller and SKU, and ordered records by effective timestamp and update timestamp in descending order, retaining the latest applicable record. For overlapping promotions, I applied the highest-priority promotion rule; where priority was equal, I selected the most recently updated valid record. I also flagged ambiguous cases separately rather than silently forcing a decision. This gave us a reliable product-level view and helped isolate the recurring pricing-error pattern.
