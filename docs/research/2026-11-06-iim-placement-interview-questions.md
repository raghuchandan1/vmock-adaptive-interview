# What IIM students are asked in placement interviews (Indian B-school context)

Research note, 6 November 2026. Purpose: check whether the adaptive interviewer's generated questions match what recruiters actually ask MBA students at IIM Mumbai (formerly NITIE Mumbai) in summer-internship and final placements.

Scope: placement interviews at Indian B-schools in general (IIMs and peers such as XLRI and MDI), which is where nearly all the evidence comes from. IIM Mumbai, the first release, has its own section (batch mix, recruiters); where it differs from other IIMs (more operations), the note says so.

## Summary

- **No IIM Mumbai / NITIE placement-interview account contains a recruiter's actual question.** We found 4 first-hand NITIE accounts (all operations, 2020-22). They describe the format (GD, then a supply-chain knowledge interview, then an HR interview) but quote no questions. Everything else comes from other IIMs and B-schools (IIM Lucknow, Bangalore, Ahmedabad, Calcutta, Indore, Ranchi, Shillong, XLRI, MDI), mostly 2015-2022.
- **Much of what the web calls "IIM interview questions" is about admission interviews, not placements.** This includes low-CGPA, gap-year, budget and current-affairs questions, and the common "why MBA" lists. We kept these out of the placement evidence. A short separate note covers them (see "Admission-interview material" below).
- **Across all domains the placement interview has the same core:**
  - A CV and work-experience deep-dive. Interviewers test "how well do you know what you claim to know".
  - "Why this domain / why this company".
  - A domain-specific problem:
    - Consulting: a case.
    - Analytics, consulting, finance and FMCG: a guesstimate.
    - Finance: technical questions.
    - Marketing: a brand or go-to-market question.
    - Product management: a product design or root-cause question.
    - Operations: an ops caselet. This is prep-guide evidence only.
- **Many recruiters run a group discussion (GD) first, before any interview.** Examples are HUL, Marico (a case discussion), Mondelez, ICICI, Axis, Capgemini, the Tata Administrative Services (TAS) "Chairman's GD", and Mahindra.
- **IIM Mumbai's batch profile matters.** In the batch graduating in 2025, 24% were freshers, average work experience was 18 months, and 53% of prior work experience was in operations/supply chain ([official report](https://iimmumbai.ac.in/placement/storage/files/Placement_Report_Batch_of_2025.pdf)). Interviewers set cases and questions from the sector on the CV, so most IIM Mumbai students should expect an operations-flavoured work-experience grilling, whatever the role.
- **Summer and final interviews differ:**
  - Summer interviews are short: about 20 minutes per consulting interview, against about 45 minutes in finals.
  - Final-placement interviews add questions about the summer internship.
  - TAS formally makes finals candidates present their summer project.

### Evidence labels used in this note

- **[First-hand, read]:** a student's own account. We read the page or PDF text. Quotes are verbatim or near-verbatim, because some pages passed through a summariser.
- **[First-hand, unverified]:** a candidate review on Glassdoor or AmbitionBox, seen only as a search-engine summary because the site blocked automated fetches (HTTP 403). These are paraphrases, not quotes, and are not in quotation marks. Check them before using any in the product.
- **[Recruiter official]:** the firm's own careers or interview page, read by us.
- **[Secondary]:** prep sites, B-school club guides and casebooks, and coaching sellers. The host is named where it matters.
- **Dates:** most first-hand material is 3-10 years old. Treat formats as indicative.

## IIM Mumbai context (official reports)

- **Programmes:** MBA (General Management), MBA in Operations & Supply Chain Management, and MBA in Sustainability Management ([2026 final report](https://iimmumbai.ac.in/placement/storage/files/Placement_Report_Batch_of_2026.pdf)).
- **Batch of 2025** ([final placement report](https://iimmumbai.ac.in/placement/storage/files/Placement_Report_Batch_of_2025.pdf)):
  - 479 students.
  - Work experience: 24% freshers, 20% 1-12 months, 33% 13-24 months, 14% 25-36 months, 9% over 36 months. Average 18 months.
  - Prior work experience by sector: Operations & Supply Chain 53%, IT/Analytics/Product 14%, Consulting 12%, BFSI 7%, Others 14%. This chart has no freshers slice, so these are shares of students with work experience. Ops/SCM is roughly 40% of the whole batch.
  - The summer report for the same 2023-25 batch (next item) gives slightly different figures: 20% freshers instead of 24%, and Ops/SCM as 34% of the whole batch.
  - Final offers by sector: Consulting 23%, Manufacturing/Process 17%, E-com & Retail 14%, IT/Tech 11%, BFSI 10%, FMCG 10%, Others 9%, Pharma 6%.
  - 144 PPOs.
  - Recruiters:
    - Consulting: Accenture Strategy, A&M, Deloitte, EY, EY Parthenon, Kearney, KPMG, PwC, ZS, Miebach.
    - FMCG: HUL, ITC, P&G, Nestlé, Marico, Diageo and others.
    - Manufacturing: Asian Paints, Bajaj Auto, Hero, Mercedes-Benz, Reliance, Vedanta and others.
    - E-com: Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, Zepto, Blinkit, Meesho.
    - Logistics, listed under Others: Delhivery, DTDC, Maersk, Allcargo, Mahindra Logistics, Shadowfax.
- **Summer batch 2023-25** ([SIP report](https://iimmumbai.ac.in/storage/pages/placement/SIP_report_batch_2025.pdf)):
  - 480 students, 20% freshers.
  - Work-experience sectors: Operations/SCM 34%, IT/Analytics 17%, Consulting 9%, Sales/Marketing 4%.
  - 165 companies.
  - The consulting list includes McKinsey. TAS is listed among the recruiters.
- **Batch of 2026** ([final report](https://iimmumbai.ac.in/placement/storage/files/Placement_Report_Batch_of_2026.pdf)):
  - 208 recruiters, 179 PPOs.
  - The sector page appears to show Consulting 25%, BFSI 13%, FMCG 12%, IT & Analytics 10% and E-com 7%. Our text extraction of the layout is partly scrambled.
  - Recruiters include Bain Capability Network, Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, Citi, Nomura and Tata Administrative Services.
  - The role split (Sales & Marketing 30%, Consulting 23%, Supply Chain 22%, BFSI 13%, IT/Tech/Analytics 7%) is [Cracku's reading](https://cracku.in/iim-mumbai-final-placements-2026/) of the chart, not text we could verify.
- **What this means:**
  - MBB presence is light: McKinsey in summers, Bain's capability network in finals.
  - Consulting volume comes from Accenture, the Big Four, Kearney, A&M and ZS.
  - Sales & marketing, supply chain and consulting are the three big role families.
- **Process:** final placements are split into a lateral process for candidates with over about 20 months of experience and a regular process for freshers ([Careers360 via search summary](https://bschool.careers360.com/articles/iim-mumbai-placements-2025), secondary, not confirmed on the institute site). The official reports do not describe the interview-day process.

## 1. Operations / Supply Chain

**Evidence: weak.** We found 4 first-hand NITIE accounts. None quotes a recruiter question. There are no first-hand MBA accounts for manufacturing (Reliance, Bajaj, Hero, Mercedes, Vedanta) or logistics (Delhivery, DTDC).

### Format

- **RPSG FMCG (TooYumm), ops summer internship that became a PPO, NITIE:** a GD, then "2 rounds of interviews". One round "focused on supply chain knowledge" and the other was an "HR round" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/prepare-operations-internship)) [First-hand, read].
- **Freudenberg Gala, Operations & Supply Chain, NITIE 2021:** "group discussion first followed by personal interview… 2 rounds in immediate sequence". Results came the same day ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-freudenberg-ayush-garg-nitie-mumbai)) [First-hand, read].
- **Asian Paints management trainee, final placement, NITIE (PGDISEM programme):** "more like a conversation than an interview". It covered MBA subjects of interest, the impact of the internship, and situational questions on analytical and project-management skills ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/5-things-that-i-followed-to-crack-my-dream-job-at-asian-paints-ft-divyanshu-rai-management-trainee)). [First-hand, read; the article is tagged sponsored.]
- **Amazon India Ops, NITIE 2022:** only the intro loaded. The student credits NITIE's first-module Operations course for the concepts ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-amazon-3)) [First-hand, read, no questions].
- **HUL supply-chain trainee:** a resume shortlist, then a GD on real HUL supply-chain problems, then a 40-50 minute PI. The PI went deep on projects and past internships, then core subjects and case questions ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Hindustan-Unilever-Interview-Questions-E9399737.htm)). [First-hand, unverified; campus probably an engineering school.]

### Question types and examples

- **Ops caselet (GD case):** HUL takes about 6 months and a 20-ton minimum batch to launch a product, while start-ups launch in 1 month at 1 ton. What would you change in procurement, manufacturing and distribution? (HUL, [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Hindustan-Unilever-Interview-Questions-E9399737.htm)) [First-hand, unverified]
- **Internship and accounts:** Asian Paints supply-chain candidates were asked about their internship and had a round on accounts ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Asian-Paints-Supply-Chain-Interview-Questions-EI_IE250495.0,12_KO13,25.htm)) [First-hand, unverified].
- **Behavioural, Amazon Leadership Principles style:** tell me about a time you analysed data; about a time you were innovative at work; about a time you changed your manager's or client's mind (Amazon MBA summer, [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Amazon-MBA-Summer-Interview-Questions-EI_IE6036.0,6_KO7,17.htm)) [First-hand, unverified].
- **Prep-guide domain questions** (not traced to an IIM interview):
  - "What is EOQ and what is its main weakness?" ([tixpapa](https://tixpapa.com/tools/interview/operations-and-supply-chain-interview-questions))
  - "You need to reduce supply chain costs by 15%"
  - "what is Kaizen, and how would you apply it in a supply chain context?" ([Board Infinity](https://www.boardinfinity.com/blog/supply-chain-management-operations-interview-questions/))
  - All [Secondary].

### What interviewers look for

- **The InsideIIM ops prep guide** (Rutul Shah) lists five areas: "HR prep, GD prep, Guesstimates, Operations Caselets, and Domain Prep".
  - Suggested order: guesstimates, then cost-side profitability caselets, then company cases "asked in Amazon, Uber, Myntra".
  - Ops roles "generally… require some degree of past work experience or background in the given domain" ([InsideIIM guide](https://insideiim.com/summer-placement-preparation-complete-guide)) [Secondary].
- **The NITIE RPSG student** credits "my experience, analytical skills, and supply chain knowledge" and GD communication. Prep sources: Chopra-Meindl ("the bible of Supply Chain at NITIE"), Victor Cheng, Case in Point. Excel is "the lifeline of any Operations Manager" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/prepare-operations-internship)) [First-hand, read].
- **Metrics and topics an IIM Kozhikode careers deck says to know:**
  - Topics: forecasting, inventory, network design, lean/Six Sigma.
  - Metrics: OTIF, fill rate, safety stock, reorder point, ABC.
  - E-commerce: cost per shipment, SLA breaches.
  - Expect walkthroughs of past cost-saving and lead-time projects.
  - Source: [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/iimk-cc-om-scm/237259934) [Secondary].
- **On-ground work is real:** Asian Paints' B-school trainee programme includes a "rigorous 3-month induction" with "night shifts at the shopfloor" ([Asian Paints careers](https://careers.asianpaints.com/content/Student-Program/?locale=en_GB)) [Recruiter official]. We found no first-hand relocation question for ops roles.

## 2. Consulting

**Evidence: strongest of all domains.**
- The IIM Lucknow Consulting & Strategy Club Casebook 2022 holds about 71 student-written summer and finals accounts ([PDF, hosted by a coaching seller, MasterTheCase](https://s3.amazonaws.com/kajabi-storefronts-production/file-uploads/sites/2148113698/themes/2155865139/downloads/ea5176-5b67-26b8-4b7a-352736e7c75_MasterTheCase-Case-Interview-Casebooks-IIM-Lucknow-2022.pdf)) [First-hand, read; compiled by the club].
- 5 standalone accounts add to it.
- None is from IIM Mumbai.

### Format

- **Campus day (IIML casebook):**
  - "2-3 case interviews back-to-back".
  - "Each interview typically lasts for 45 mins for final placements while the summers process usually has shorter interviews lasting for around 20 minutes. It comprises of a case and HR questions."
  - A typical interview: "3-4 minutes of informal conversation", fit (HR) questions, questions on work experience, then a case. Case types: profitability, market entry, pricing, abstract, estimation.
  - "you are asked cases based on themes/sectors in your CV."
  - Before the day there is about a month-long alumni "buddy process" with practice cases.
  - The [IIML Finals Casebook 2025](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/csc-iim-lucknow-finals-casebook-2025-1-pdf/285938034) shows the same structure.
- **Firms' own pages:**
  - BCG: a skills interview to "explore your experience, skills, and motivation", then case interviews, then a team interview ([BCG](https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/interview-process)) [Recruiter official].
  - Bain: a digital assessment, then case interviews plus discussion of "your past experience" ([Bain](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/interviewing/)) [Recruiter official].
  - McKinsey: a personal experience interview plus a case. We could not load the page (timed out), so this is from search summaries only ([McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/careers/interviewing)).
- **Individual first-hand accounts:**
  - Deloitte, IIM Lucknow summers 2015: HR stress round, then a case with a data sheet, then a principal round on the CV and the case ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/deloitte-interview-experience-4)) [First-hand, read].
  - Deloitte USI, IIM Shillong 2021: "mini case cum guesstimate round", then "a full-blown case study", then a personal interview on domain and fit ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-deloitte-usi-akshay-krishnan-b-iim-shillong)) [First-hand, read].
  - McKinsey, IIM Ahmedabad summers: two roughly 20-minute partner rounds. One was a case; the other was a line-by-line CV grilling with no case ([Mentoresult](https://www.mentoresult.com/blog/how-was-my-interview-experience-in-management-consulting-at-mckinsey/)). [First-hand, read; the author sells coaching.]

### Question types and examples

All are [First-hand, read] from the IIML casebook unless another source is linked.

**Fit, "why consulting" and CV**
- "Why shift to consulting after engineering?" (Accenture Strategy)
- "Walk me through your CV and major projects done?" (A&M)
- "Why BCG?" (BCG, fresher)
- "Why consulting?" / "Take me through some of the projects you've worked on in EY" (Bain section)
- "Tell me about yourself including your family" / "What motivates you in life?" (Accenture Strategy)
- "Why did you work only for less than a year at Deloitte?" / "What was the biggest takeaway from your internship at Bain?" (finals, Accenture Strategy)
- "In your CV, I don't find any POR on campus?" / "If we give you PPO, will you be willing to travel?" (Deloitte, IIM Lucknow, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/deloitte-interview-experience-4))

**Cases**
- "Your client is one of the Top 3 IT services company. It is not growing. Find why?" (Bain)
- "Your client is a Spice Manufacturer and Seller. They have been facing stagnant revenues from past 3 years…" (Kearney)
- "Your client is a manufacturer (Assume industry of your choice), and it wants to improve its productivity. Find areas of improvements and make recommendations" (KPMG; given to a candidate with manufacturing work experience)
- "Your client is a big hospital looking to improve its operations…" (A&M)
- "Reduce price by 5%, and volume would go up by 25%. Evaluate the proposal on contribution margin basis." (McKinsey, finals)
- "A cable box manufacturer wants to outsource manufacturing." … "What are decision making criteria?" (Deloitte, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/deloitte-interview-experience-4))

**Guesstimates**
- "Guesstimate the total number of dinner sets in Lucknow." The interviewer added: "Do not worry about the numbers. Just go with the approach quickly." (Accenture Strategy)
- "Guesstimate the number of Ola and Uber cabs in Delhi." (BCG)
- "Estimate the number of 2-Wheeler Electric Vehicles for last-mile delivery of e-commerce packages in a township near Mumbai." (Accenture Strategy)

**Domain and current affairs (mostly for candidates with work experience)**
- "Which one is better? NPV vs IRR" and the candidate's stand on world events (Accenture Management Consulting, IIM Lucknow 2015, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/accenture-management-consulting-interview-experience-6))
- GST rate structure (McKinsey, IIM Bangalore, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/cracking-the-mckinsey-interview-an-iim-bangalore-students-mckinsey-interview-experience))
- "What do understand by working capital financing and what is the ideal working capital ratio for FMCG companies." (finals, IIML casebook)

### What interviewers look for

- **IIML casebook:**
  - "communication skills (clarity of thought, articulation & crisp communication), problem solving skills (scoping the problem… structured approach and asking the right questions) and numeric skills… presence & personality".
  - In HR questions, "how good a fit you are for the firm".
  - In guesstimates, approach first and numbers last.
- **BCG:** integrity, intellectual curiosity, creative thinking, a collaborative mindset, drive ([BCG](https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/interview-process)).
- **Bain:** people who "can explain why they want to join us specifically" ([Bain](https://www.bain.com/careers/hiring-process/interviewing/)).

## 3. Product Management

**Evidence: weak.** We found only 2 first-hand B-school campus accounts, both IIM Bangalore and both old. The richest campus source is an IIT Madras (engineering school) PM casebook. The IIM Lucknow, IIM Calcutta and IIM Bangalore PM casebooks exist but are behind Scribd and Course Hero paywalls.

### Format

- **Microsoft IDC programme-manager intern, IIM Bangalore 2017:**
  - "Resume Shortlist > Group Discussion" (a design exercise), then "Interview Round 1 with a senior PM > Interview Round 2 with a Director". There was no HR round.
  - Freshers and candidates with work experience both got offers ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-interview-experience-microsoft-idc-program-manager-intern-role)) [First-hand, read].
- **OYO PM, IIM Bangalore alum 2019:** background, why OYO, why PM, then product case studies. "Guesstimates are generally expected as well" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/oyo-product-manager-interview-iim-bangalore-alum)) [First-hand, read].
- **Amazon PM (global, not campus-specific):**
  - A 60-minute screen, half Leadership-Principle behavioural questions and half functional PM questions.
  - Then a writing assessment and five 55-minute interviews.
  - "Use the STAR method"; "We won't ask brain teasers" ([Amazon](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/product-manager-interview-prep)) [Recruiter official].
- **Amazon summers at a B-school:** a decision-ranking simulation as the elimination round, then a technical interview, then a discussion ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/prepare-e-commerce-roles-summer-placements)) [Secondary: a friend's account].

### Question types and examples

- **Design and feature:**
  - "pick any MS Office product and come up with a feature for it"
  - "design a feature palette for Paytm"
  - Both from Microsoft, IIM Bangalore ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-interview-experience-microsoft-idc-program-manager-intern-role)) [First-hand, read].
- **Favourite product:**
  - "What is your favourite product?" then "what is one pain point associated with it and how would you solve it?" (Groww, from the IIT Madras casebook, [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/the-iit-madras-product-management-casebook-23-24-pdf/270098109)) [First-hand, read, not a B-school].
- **Root-cause analysis:**
  - "the order cancellations on Flipkart have increased by 20%." (Groww)
  - "Time spent by users on YouTube Shorts has been decreasing over the last 3 weeks"
  - Both from the IIT Madras casebook.
- **Strategy:**
  - "Amazon wants to do drone delivery in India. How would you go about doing that?"
  - "Estimate the number of autos near IIM"
  - Both from an XLRI PM careers deck ([SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/product-management-career-conversation-xlri/238479407)) [Secondary].
- **CV and technical:**
  - Work experience and "how did I bring a difference"
  - Basic sorting and database questions
  - Demonetisation
  - All from Microsoft, IIM Bangalore [First-hand, read].

### What interviewers look for

- **OYO account:** "An overall structure to the way I ended up designing the product and my justifications for various product decisions" [First-hand, read].
- **Microsoft account:** repeated probing of "the rationale behind picking that particular feature" [First-hand, read].
- **Amazon:** answers in STAR form that "include metrics or data" and explain "the 'why' of your decisions" [Recruiter official].

## 4. Analytics / Business Analyst

**Evidence: weak for MBA.** We found 2 first-hand MBA accounts (Tiger Analytics at IIM Raipur, Capgemini at IIM Ranchi). The rest are engineering-campus accounts or Glassdoor summaries.

### Format

- **ZS:** "online assessments, behavioral, case and subject matter expertise interviews… two to four rounds" ([ZS](https://www.zs.com/careers/interview-process)) [Recruiter official].
- **Amazon business intelligence roles:** an online assessment ("SQL Challenge, Working with SQL, and Work Styles"), a 60-minute phone screen, then "five 55-minute interviews" ([Amazon](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/bie-interview-prep)) [Recruiter official].
- **Capgemini, IIM Ranchi:**
  - A GD "with two topics, one related to the latest news and other being an abstract one".
  - A technical round "mainly based on resume, understanding about the particular business unit".
  - An HR round with "scenarios-based questions" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/a-journey-to-ppo-from-capgemini-alekhya-kakumanu-iim-ranchi)) [First-hand, read].
- **Tiger Analytics, IIM Raipur 2022:** a case with a presentation, an interview on the presentation, then HR ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-sodexo-2)) [First-hand, read].

### Question types and examples

- **CV-anchored case:** the candidate was asked to analyse the supply chain of Asian Paints, his previous employer (Tiger Analytics, IIM Raipur, [InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-sodexo-2)) [First-hand, read].
- **Official practice cases:**
  - "Help All-Star Airlines explore options to expand into new markets"
  - A pharma pricing case
  - Source: ZS ([ZS case practice](https://www.zs.com/careers/interview-process/case-interview-practice)) [Recruiter official].
- **Guesstimates and puzzles, engineering campus (EXL business analyst):**
  - "the amount of rice exported to Afghanistan by India in one fiscal year". This was tied to the candidate's stated interest in geopolitics.
  - Lucknow metro daily ridership (his hometown).
  - A bucket puzzle.
  - "the probability of getting all heads when we toss three unbiased coins"
  - Source: [GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/exl-services-interview-experience-for-business-analyst/) [First-hand, read, not a B-school].
- **From Glassdoor reviews:**
  - Media.net: intermediate SQL, puzzles and guesstimates (for example, babies born in India per day), and a root-cause question on a 25% drop in YouTube views ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Media-net-Business-Analyst-Interview-Questions-EI_IE1042683.0,9_KO10,26.htm)).
  - ZS: a guesstimate built from the candidate's CV ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/ZS-Associates-Decision-Analytics-Associate-Interview-Questions-EI_IE115506.0,13_KO14,42.htm)).
  - [First-hand, unverified]

### What interviewers look for

- **ZS:** "how you structure problems, develop solutions, apply feedback and think creatively"; "There is no perfect answer" ([ZS](https://www.zs.com/careers/interview-process)).
- **Amazon:** SQL, "Analytical problem solving", metrics, "Business acumen"; STAR answers with data ([Amazon](https://amazon.jobs/content/en/how-we-hire/bie-interview-prep)).
- **Guesstimates:** scored on approach, not the final number ([InsideIIM guesstimate guide](https://insideiim.com/guesstimate-questions-to-practice-for-summer-placements)) [Secondary].
- **Work-experience candidates** at Genpact were asked mostly about their work experience ([InsideIIM, SCMHRD 2021](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-experience-2021-genpact-purojit-mitra-scmhrd-pune)) [First-hand, unverified: seen only as a search summary].

## 5. Finance

**Evidence: moderate.** We found 7 first-hand B-school placement accounts (IIM Lucknow, Bangalore, Calcutta, Ranchi, Kashipur). None is from IIM Mumbai. There are no first-hand accounts for Kotak, HDFC, Citi, BofA, Nomura, CRISIL or NPCI at a named B-school.

### Format

- **Investment banks:**
  - J.P. Morgan IB, IIM Bangalore: "a total of 2 rounds of interview (each 30-40 mins)", mostly valuation ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/how-to-be-an-investment-banker)) [First-hand, read].
  - JPMC Markets, IIM Lucknow 2016: "Two with seniors from the team and one with the HR" ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/jp-morgan-chase-at-iim-lucknow-summer-internship-interview-experiences-chapter-5)) [First-hand, read].
- **Indian banks often run a GD before the PI:**
  - ICICI Bank, IIM Ranchi: "a case based GD", then the personal interview ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/how-i-bagged-a-ppo-from-iim-ranchi)).
  - Axis Bank, IIM Calcutta: a GD on payment banks, then a PI ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/summer-internship-interview-experience-at-axis-bank-niteen-bali-from-iim-calcutta)).
  - Both [First-hand, read].

### Question types and examples

- **Corporate finance and accounting:**
  - "Which one is better? NPV vs IRR"
  - CAPM
  - Scenarios where the candidate had to say "what impact each event or transaction will have on Profit and loss statement, Balance sheet and Cash flow statement"
  - Source: Accenture Management Consulting, IIM Lucknow 2015, a finance-track interview ([InsideIIM](https://insideiim.com/accenture-management-consulting-interview-experience-6)) [First-hand, read].
- **Markets and risk:**
  - Questions on "a bank's risk management policy and how do they use different financial instruments to hedge their risk"
  - "a lot of questions on my personal equity portfolio"
  - Source: JPMC, IIM Lucknow [First-hand, read].
- **Valuation:** "mostly valuation related questions in very detail" and "Why Investment Banking" (JPM IB, IIM Bangalore) [First-hand, read].
- **Current affairs and policy:**
  - "Questions about GST bill, Bankruptcy Bill, Euro crisis"
  - The candidate's own blog
  - Source: Axis Bank, IIM Calcutta [First-hand, read].
- **From Glassdoor reviews:**
  - ICICI Bank summer intern ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/ICICI-Bank-Summer-Intern-Interview-Questions-EI_IE11763.0,10_KO11,24.htm)):

## Cross-domain patterns

### CV and work experience

- **The CV is the spine of every interview in every domain:**
  - Summer interviewers "test… how well do you know what you claim to know" (JPMC).
  - Consulting cases are "based on themes/sectors in your CV" (IIML casebook).
  - Analytics guesstimates are built from the candidate's CV, hometown or interests (EXL, ZS).
- **Work-experience candidates get questions on their own field:**
  - The Siemens candidate got a manufacturing productivity case (IIML casebook).
  - The former consultant was asked whether FMCG firms need consultants (HUL).
  - The candidate who had worked at Asian Paints was asked to analyse Asian Paints' supply chain (Tiger Analytics).
  - Short stints are questioned: "Why did you work only for less than a year at Deloitte?"
  - At IIM Mumbai, 53% of students with work experience come from operations/supply chain, so expect ops questions to dominate.
- **Freshers:** questions shift to academics, the first-semester project, PORs and hobbies (HUL, Marico, ICICI accounts above). Marketing and IB explicitly do not require relevant experience.

### Why MBA / why this domain / why switch

- **Placement interviews ask "why this domain" and "why this company" far more than "why MBA".** Examples: "Why shift to consulting after engineering?", "Why consulting with Accenture Strategy & not any other firm?", "Why Investment Banking", "Why BCG?", why PM / why OYO, and "Why I wanted to pursue marketing?". Pure "why MBA" is mostly an admission-interview question.

### Summer internship in final placements, and PPO/PPI

- **Final-placement interviews probe the internship:** "What was the biggest takeaway from your internship at Bain?" and "Which module was driven by you?" (IIML casebook finals).
- **TAS formally requires a presentation of the summer project** in finals and for the PPO ([tata.com](https://www.tata.com/careers/programs/tas/tas-campus-tata-group)).
- **PPOs are decided by internship reviews**, not interviews: mid-term and final presentations to the department head (ICICI, ICICI Lombard, Capgemini accounts above).
- **PPIs come from competitions,** for example ITC Interrobang ([ITC careers](https://itcportal.com/careers.html)) [Recruiter official].
- **Not found:** first-hand evidence of the question "why didn't you get a PPO?".

### Group discussion

- **A GD or case discussion is the first filter** at HUL, Marico, Mondelez, ICICI, Axis, Capgemini, TAS, Mahindra and ABG, and at the NITIE ops recruiters (RPSG, Freudenberg).

### Relocation, on-ground roles, current affairs

- **Relocation / travel:**
  - "If we give you PPO, will you be willing to travel?" (Deloitte) [First-hand, read]
  - Willingness to travel anywhere in India and a rural sales plan (ITC) [First-hand, unverified]
  - Asian Paints' shopfloor night shifts [Recruiter official]
- **Current affairs:**
  - Appear in finance (GST, the Bankruptcy Bill), consulting (GST, world events), PM (demonetisation) and IT-consulting GDs (Capgemini).
  - Usually tied to the candidate's field.

### Salary

- **Not investigated.** We found no evidence either way on whether salary is discussed in campus interviews.

### Admission-interview material (kept separate)

- **Not placement evidence:** IIM admission interview reports, including IIM Mumbai's own ([Cracku](https://cracku.in/iim-mumbai-interview-experiences/)).
  - Example: an OSCM applicant asked "Why supply chain" and about the book *The Goal*.
- **Most web material on low CGPA, gap years, "why MBA after engineering" and budget/current-affairs questions comes from these admission interviews.**
- **Placement-specific evidence on CGPA and gaps** is limited to the unverified ICICI item above.

## Implications for an AI mock interviewer

1. **CV and work-experience deep-dive is the must-have.** Every domain does it.
   - Follow-ups should test whether the student really knows each CV claim: what they personally did, the numbers, what changed.
   - About half of students with work experience (roughly 40% of the whole batch) come from ops/SCM jobs, so the interviewer needs good operations follow-ups (plant, inventory, vendor, cost-saving projects) even for consulting or marketing roles.
   - It should also question short stints and switches ("why did you leave after a year?").
2. **Add "why this domain / why this company / why switch from engineering or your last field".** This is more placement-relevant than "why MBA", and nearly universal.
3. **Guesstimates are needed.** They appear in consulting, analytics, finance (CRISIL, IDFC) and FMCG marketing (HUL).
   - Score and probe the approach (segmentation, assumptions, sanity check), not the final number.
   - Interviewers personalise them to the CV, hometown or interests.
4. **Short cases or caselets are needed for consulting, and help for ops, analytics and marketing.**
   - Summer-length cases run about 20 minutes. Final-length cases run about 45 minutes.
   - Case types: profitability, market entry, pricing, operations improvement, and cases built from the sector on the CV.
5. **Domain-knowledge questions per track:**
   - Finance: NPV vs IRR, CAPM, the three statements, valuation basics.
   - Marketing: STP/4Ps, brand comparison, launch/GTM, category extension.
   - PM: product design, favourite-product improvement, root-cause metrics.
   - Ops: EOQ, safety stock, OTIF, lean. This list rests on prep guides, not recruiter quotes, so treat it as provisional.
6. **A summer-internship deep-dive for final-placement mode.** Ask what the student owned, the impact, the biggest takeaway, and a structured project presentation (TAS does this formally).
7. **Behavioural and ethics questions:** conflict, created something new, ethical dilemma, leadership and POR. These come from P&G, TAS, Amazon and McKinsey-style personal experience interviews.
8. **Short current-affairs questions tied to the student's field** (GST, RBI, the sector on the CV). Use these sparingly.
9. **Relocation and on-ground willingness** (travel, rural sales, plant shifts) for sales and ops roles. Ask this lightly; evidence is thin.
10. **Out of scope for a one-to-one voice interviewer:** group discussions and chairman's GDs. These are a common first filter, so tell students about them, but we cannot simulate them.
11. **Separate fresher and work-experience question mixes.** Freshers get academics, the first-semester project, PORs and hobbies. Work-experience candidates get cases from their own sector.
